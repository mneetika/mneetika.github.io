I am a Senior Software Engineer at Meta in London, focused on iOS, real-time media, Generative AI, augmented reality, camera experiences, and mobile product engineering.

At Meta, I work on iOS product and platform features across video calling, screen sharing, camera infrastructure, Generative AI-powered virtual backgrounds, Live AI experiences for smart glasses, and WhatsApp augmented reality effects. This includes work on call latency, playback reliability, and the quality of real-time communication experiences.

Previously, I was a founding engineer on Uber's Mobile Payments team in India, where I built payment systems for Uber Rides and Uber Eats across global markets. I have also worked on enterprise collaboration and email products at Citrix, marketplace experiences at OLX, and multiple iOS applications across telecom, media, commerce, gaming, and productivity domains.

I hold a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering from Jamia Hamdard University, New Delhi, where I graduated as a Gold Medalist.
